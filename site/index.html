GraviApps

Version 1.0.0-8 Latest
By renlovecomputer · iphoneos-arm64 · Rootless

Shake your iPhone and let Home Screen icons fall into a gravity-based physics simulation. Tilt the device to make them move across the screen.

  • Gravity, bouncing, and icon collisions.
  • Device tilt controls gravity direction.
  • Battery Saving and Normal performance modes.
  • Second shake restores the original icon layout.

GlyphTime

Version 1.1.8 Latest
By renlovecomputer · iphoneos-arm64 · Rootless

Build a custom Lock Screen clock from individual image glyphs. Choose separate artwork for every digit and the colon.

  • 11 independent image slots (0–9 and colon).
  • Import PNG, JPG, JPEG, HEIC and animated GIF images.
  • Optional GIF playback, image scaling, and clock tint.
  • The native clock remains visible until all glyphs are ready.
